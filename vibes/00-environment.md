Jog modes: 0=relative, 1=absolute continuous (±4096 ≈ half turn, baseline = position when mode set), 2=relative, 3=absolute deadzone. 44 keys (STOP_PLAY 0x3c, JOG 0x1d, CAM1–9, IN/OUT …).
